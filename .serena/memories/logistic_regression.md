# Logistic Regression Lab (`logistic-regression`)

- **5 linear stages** (`src/features/logistic-regression/domain/stages.ts`): `1. strategy` -> `2. tokenize` -> `3. score` -> `4. train` -> `5. experience`.
  - Stage 1 (`strategy.ts`): 10-word intuition strategy with 5 discrete polarity tiers (`[-2, 2]` integer weights: `−2 强正常`..`+2 强垃圾`), 6 10-word SMS examples, and signed risk score cutoff `w·x >= +1` (deliberately no synthetic probabilities; requires both positive and negative polarity choices without polluting neutral words).
    The six results and first SMS calculation are read-only, derived from weights; Stage 1 drops `selectedMsgId` on draft load, while Stage 3 retains selection for the Sigmoid explanation.
  - Stage 3 (`ScorePanel.tsx`, `state.ts`): Starts from a prefilled spam blacklist (`b = +0.4`, spam weights pre-set, normal words `0`) with a live Sigmoid S-curve SVG visualizer (`z = w·x + b -> σ(z)`), focusing students on why `b < 0` and negative counter-weights are needed for probabilistic calibration.
- **Curriculum migration** (`server/db/client.ts`): v17 shifted the old five stages forward for the strategy intro. v19 keeps stages 1–4, archives retired evaluation submissions at index 7, maps former stage-6 passes to stage 5, and bumps project generation. `current_stage = 6` is the completion sentinel. v20 renames the lab ID across projects, submissions, and both visibility tables; old browser links redirect in `src/app/router.tsx`. v21 removes old experience drafts and revisions and strips `probeText` from historical evaluation snapshots.
- **Corpus split & classroom scrubbing** (`domain/corpusData.ts`): 1,400 training + 300 validation SMS rows, PII-redacted (`TEL`/`URL`/`ID`/`NUM`), stored as `<label>\t<text>`. Vocabulary comes only from the training split. The retired held-out corpus was removed.
- **Unified engine**:
  - `tokenizeWords` (`domain/tokenizer.ts`) performs forward-maximum-matching against the fixed `CN_LEXICON` (`cnLexicon.ts`, MIT general lexicon, independent of `trainVocab`). Corpus ingestion and UI inspection share this tokenizer so model predictions and explanations agree.
  - `trainLogisticRegression` (`logisticRegression.ts`) uses seeded per-epoch Fisher-Yates SGD shuffling (`DEFAULT_TRAIN_SEED = 2026`, `shuffleEpochs: true` default) alongside invariant optional `bgdHistory`.
- **Assessment & experience**: Concepts are graded only in stages 1–3. Stage 4 grades training and validation BCE; its math derivation is optional. Stage 5 reconstructs the latest passing stage-4 snapshot with a draft fallback for previews, and selects the cutoff by public validation F1 over 5–95%. Free-text SMS stay in `ExperiencePanel` state, and stage 5 rejects draft writes. Completion checks the model's prediction for one public example and stores only the completion marker.
- **Experiment comparison**: Stage 4 `ui/ExperimentComparison.tsx` keeps one component-local observation of public results, independent of draft/autosave/judge payloads; leaving the stage clears it. Parameter-change hints exclude replay epoch and BGD visibility. Intra-epoch plots show validation-loss checkpoints (10 per epoch for the full training set), not every SGD update.
- **Staff mode vs student classroom mode & pinned URL stage** (`lesson/state.ts`, `ui/LogisticRegressionLabPage.tsx`): Staff (`teacher`/`admin` via `useLabProject({ staff })`) receive permanent `staffMode` free stage navigation, direct `/judge` submission, and shared `LabPageShell` self-reset without the student classroom toggle. For students (`role === "user"`), `?classroom=1&stage=N` or the rail toggle enables `classroomMode` preview while keeping formal judge submission gated by `logisticRegressionStageUnlocked`. `pinnedStage` (`number | null`) distinguishes explicit `stage=1` from omitted `stage` across `load-project` and clears on `isReset`.
- **Corpus split cache & deferred training consistency** (`logisticRegression.ts`, `TrainPanel`): `getPreparedPublicSplit(trainCount)` caches vocab and vectorized public splits; panels using `useDeferredValue` guard submission during `isStale` and bind metrics/comparison records to settled `run.*` parameters.
- **Stage 4 order & optional 1D slice** (`TrainPanel`, `LossDerivationCard`, `lossSlice.ts`, `WeightLossSliceExplorer`): Auto-training + 3-baseline validation comparison (all-zero, `run.baselineAcc` majority class with train-split `run.trainSpamRate` constant-prior BCE, and trained model comparing accuracy and BCE independently) precedes single-step explanation, collapsed derivation appendix (maximum likelihood → mean BCE; chain rule cancellation → single-sample gradient), and collapsed 1D slice explorer (`ContextScopedState` resets trail when `(word, contextMode, trainedModel)` changes).
